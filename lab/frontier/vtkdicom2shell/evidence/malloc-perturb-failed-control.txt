W9 2026-11-25 — MALLOC_PERTURB_ heap-origin diagnostic: FAILED CONTROL, no inference

Question: is the fatal stale `Value.V` word at the OOB slot (dereferenced by
`vtkDICOMValue::operator=` at Value.h:426, SIGSEGV in
`vtkDICOMReferenceCount::operator--`) free-list/allocator data, or a header
written by the program? A local malloc-perturbation knob should paint freed
and reallocated heap bytes; if the OOB word changed with the paint byte it
would be allocator reuse data, otherwise it could be program-written.
