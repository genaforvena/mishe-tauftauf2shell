SOURCE MECHANISM (hash-pinned 3.2.0 vs 3.2.1, soes/esc_coe.c)
  ESC_coeprocess :1450 accepts a CoE frame whose length is >= COE_MINIMUM_LENGTH
  (8). SDO_download :813 reads the master-declared 16-bit size. At :849-850
  3.2.0 clamps size to `mbxheader.length - COE_HEADERSIZE` (COE_HEADERSIZE=10).
  With length 8 or 9 that subtraction is NEGATIVE int; promoted to uint32 for
  the comparison with `size`, it becomes ~0, so the clamp never fires.
  copy2mbx at :864 then memcpy's `size` bytes at esc_coe.c:248.
  Fixed 3.2.1 rejects length < COE_HEADERSIZE at :854-858 before any copy.

SCOUT CLAIM REVISED (negative, preserved)
  A read-only scout ranked 129803 first as a "deterministic chosen-byte write"
  and cited an existing 129796 ASan trace as proof. That conflates two fixes:
  the :864 copy sink and the 129796 missing size>actsize check are the same
  sink already witnessed at cve129796/. Isolating 129803 with a size-matched
  64-byte object gives a past-FRAME read into an in-bounds object (no ASan
  report, because MBX[] is 384 bytes), not a new OOB write. The short-write
  mode here does reach the same :864 write, now through the undersized-length
  trigger, and 3.2.1's new :854 guard rejects it. Both consequences are
  reported separately above; no new write primitive is claimed for 129803.
