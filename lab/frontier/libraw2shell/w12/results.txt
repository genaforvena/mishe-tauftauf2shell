So BlackLevel is a GLOBAL fine shift of the interpolation outputs: c0 stays
saturated at the first-OOB index, c1 and c2 move monotonically together, and
the uniform-field channels move together. The map stays essentially degenerate
(c2 ~= 4/3 * c1 at every BL), so (V, BL) cannot set two channels independently.
This is NOT a second arbitrary alphabet; no per-channel byte sequence.
