freerdp2shell wake 14 — natural post-write log trigger on the clobbered rdpsnd
log object (CVE-2026-114045 / GHSA-3m9q-g533-rqjq)

Question left open by wake 13: the chosen-pointer dereference needed a
scratch-injected WLog_INFO(TAG, ...) immediately after the real CopyMemory.
Does an unmodified FreeRDP log call, reached by an ordinary client PDU after
the corruption, dereference the same clobbered object?

Answer: YES, observed in the live stock-allocator build.

All runs: pinned FreeRDP 3.32.0 d27a4f7c..., committed five wake8 patches,
plain no-sanitizer build in /tmp/fr-wake7-shadow-build, freerdp-shadow-cli on
127.0.0.1 ports 23221-23223 with -auth /log-level:DEBUG, Sample client
/sec:rdp /u:test /p:test /sound. No off-loopback probe. Committed replay
patches, verify_live.sh and their pinned digests were NOT changed.

Scratch-only changes (restored after capture):
1. server/shadow/shadow_client.c: the synthetic 16384-byte playback buffer is
   memset 0xDE as before, with data[8904..8911] = 0x41. No server-side log
   call was added: channels/rdpsnd/server/rdpsnd_main.c is the unmodified
   committed wake-9 trace build.
2. channels/rdpsnd/client/rdpsnd_main.c: Sleep(2500) after sending the second
   SNDC_FORMATS PDU, before returning to rdpsnd_recv_server_audio_formats_pdu.
   This lets FreeRDP's own rdpsnd_send_quality_mode_pdu() SNDC_QUALITYMODE
   PDU arrive after the synthetic playback. No third PDU was crafted in the
   reported run (port 23222); the first exploratory run on port 23221 did
   craft one and is superseded by the port-23222 result.

--- honest boundary ---
The log trigger is natural code on both sides (client SNDC_QUALITYMODE, server
WLog_DBG on the rdpsnd TAG), but a scratch client-side delay was needed to
place it after the synthetic playback in this replay. Chosen bytes still come
from the lab's server-side synthetic playback, not a remote client. The wLog
object was found after the fact, not groomed. WLOG_FILTER, post-auth /auth,
subsystem-supplied format table and loopback-only remain required. No commit
to replay patches, verify_live.sh or digests; scratch sources restored and
rebuilt afterwards.
